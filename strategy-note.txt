STRATEGY

The line targets the freshest angle the rules explicitly flag as under-used:
blaming production itself rather than the usual "works on my machine" / "merge
conflict" / "timezone" gags. It plays a senior engineer who refuses to accept
the bug exists - the humor lands hardest for developers who have said exactly
this to a furious client.

Chosen after rejecting:
- "works on my machine" (overused)
- "the prod bug is just a feature that escaped QA" (overused)
- timezone gag (decent but not fresh enough)
- "if a junior writes the code, a senior must own the bug" (too on-the-nose)
